About Backroom

Backroom is a township rental platform focused on helping people find affordable accommodation in Durban's township communities.

We provide information about available rooms and houses, including their location, rental price, number of rooms and available facilities.

Our Purpose

Finding affordable accommodation can be difficult, especially when information about available rental spaces is spread across different sources.

Backroom aims to make this process easier by bringing rental information together in one simple and accessible platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make affordable township accommodation easier to discover by providing clear and useful information about rental opportunities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted platform for discovering affordable rental accommodation within township communities in Durban.

Who We Serve

  • Individuals looking for affordable rooms
  • Couples looking for rental accommodation
  • Families looking for houses
  • Students and young workers
  • People relocating within Durban
