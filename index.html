Welcome to Backroom

Backroom is a township rental platform focused on helping people find affordable accommodation in communities around Durban.

Our website provides information about available rooms and houses, including their location, size, rental price and available facilities.

Accommodation Options

Backroom provides information about different types of affordable township accommodation.

  • Single Backrooms: Simple one-room accommodation for individuals.
  • Two-Room Units: Larger accommodation suitable for individuals or couples.
  • Family Houses: Larger homes suitable for families.
